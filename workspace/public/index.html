Team workspace

Sign in with the GitHub account you registered with. Your team, your repository, the worked examples for your challenge and the countdown are all behind here.

Sign in with GitHub

No GitHub account? It is free and takes about two minutes at github.com/signup. GitHub requires you to be 13 or over and is a separate company with its own terms.

What this asks GitHub for

Two read-only permissions: your public profile, and which teams you are in. That is how the workspace knows who you are and which repository is yours.

It does not ask for access to your code. The workspace never writes to your repository — you push with your own git client, as you would on any other project. You can withdraw access at any time in your GitHub settings, under Applications, and it takes effect immediately.

What applies while you are here

You accepted the first two when you registered. Signing in does not ask you to agree to anything new — it confirms these still apply to what you do in the workspace.
